This series of articles will guide you through the process of setting a basic [Proxmox VE](https://www.proxmox.com/en/proxmox-ve) cluster, from the initial planning stages to the final configuration and management of virtual machines and containers. The necessary dedicated servers will be hosted at [Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com/). It is an entry-level proposal aimed at companies or individuals that want to self-host the software required to run their operations.
