The server infrastructure category covers all articles related to physical and virtual resources that support the management of servers in an IT environment. Specifically, these articles will be aimed at a Proxmox Virtual Environment cluster on dedicated servers at Hetzner.

Monitoring tools, such as Prometheus, Loki, Grafana and GoAccess will be included in this category, as well as applications run inside virtual machines and containers to provide Internet services, such as Tinyproxy, and database servers, such as PostgreSQL.
