---
title: "Streaming SIMD Extensions 4.2"
date: 2025-08-06
---

Streaming SIMD Extensions 4.2 (SSE4.2) are an extension of the original SSE instruction set, adding new features and capabilities for performing SIMD operations on both integer and floating-point data. They are designed to improve performance in applications that require high computational power, such as scientific simulations, financial modeling, and machine learning.
