title: "GNU Privacy Guard"
date: 2026-11-09
---

GNU Privacy Guard (GPG) is a free, open-source implementation of the OpenPGP standard (RFC 4880), distributed under the GPL license. It combines symmetric-key cryptography (for speed) with public-key cryptography (for secure key exchange), allowing users to encrypt and digitally sign files, messages, and emails so that only the intended recipient can read the content, while any recipient can verify the message's integrity and the sender's identity.
