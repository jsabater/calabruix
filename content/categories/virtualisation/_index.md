The virtualisation category covers all articles related to technologies that allow dividing of physical computing resources into a series of virtual machines, operating systems, processes or containers.

These articles will mostly dwell in the Proxmox Virtual Environment ecosystem, both using virtual machines and LinuX Containers, but hey also include backup procedures and techniques.
